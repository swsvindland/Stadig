## Calls made for correcting foods and Today's order (2026-11-03)

- **A correction, not a copy.** A catalog food's numbers are kept as a `FoodCorrection` under the catalog's own id rather than as a custom food beside it, so the barcode, search, favourites, Log Again and the entries already logged all find the corrected food in its place, without a second row to tell apart; one record per food (its id comes from the food's), so correcting it on two devices can't make two. ## Calls made for meal timing and fasting (2026-10-19)

You asked for Zero-style fasts (a circle, timers, the phase you're in and what's likely happening), for time between meals with 3–5 hours as the aim, and for fasts from a 12-hour night to several days, built "with your defaults". The five defaults proposed and taken:

- **Grazing gets the nudge; long gaps don't.** Gaps under 2 hours are flagged (orange) and lead the week's advice once at least three are and they're a third of the gaps; 3–5 hours is green; over 5 hours is neutral, except that in a lean bulk or recomp the advice asks for 3–5 meals with about 0.4 g/kg of protein when fewer than three a day reach it. The evidence for spacing is mostly protein (Areta 2013, Schoenfeld & Aragon 2018); against grazing, snacking between meals raising liver fat where the same calories with meals didn't (Koopman 2014). Open: whether 2–3 hours should be orange too.
- **Phases as ranges, no autophagy claims.** Digesting 0–4 h, insulin low 4–12 h, fat burning 12–24 h, ketosis 1–3 days, deep ketosis 3 days on, each with a sentence of what's reasonably established in people, and a caveat that the hours move with the last meal and training, and that hour-by-hour autophagy comes from mice. Open: moving the phases by the last meal's carbs or a training session (the data to do it is there; the evidence for how far isn't).
- **How long.** Presets 13, 16, 18, 24, 36, 48 and 72 hours, any goal from 12 hours to 7 days on the stepper. Goals over a day need the safety notes read once (who shouldn't, when to stop); until then nothing sets one, not even the stepper on a running fast. Over 3 days adds "best done with a doctor". Under 18 or at a BMI under 18.5, nothing over a day is offered. Siri and the Watch start fasts of up to a day only. Open: whether 7 days is the right ceiling, or 72 hours.
- **What counts as a meal.** 50 kcal: below that (black coffee, tea, a splash of milk) a moment isn't a meal, doesn't end a fast and doesn't start a gap. Studies of eating occasions use the same line. A fast ends at the first meal logged after it began, on any device; Undo of that meal puts it back. Food added within 45 minutes of the meal a fast began from (dessert after dinner) moves the fast's start to after it instead of ending it. Open: a broth-and-electrolytes allowance for long fasts.
- **Off by default.** "Meal timing and fasting" is a switch in Settings (like Drinks), because nudges about when to eat are wrong for some people and fasting apps are linked to disordered eating in young people. No streaks, and no score for fast length.

Other calls made building it:

- **The eating day runs 4 AM to 4 AM**, so a snack after midnight is the evening's last meal, not the next day's first.
- **Only trusted days are read.** Gaps, overnight fasts and the week's pattern come from complete or fasted days (and today so far); an overnight fast needs both days. An unlogged snack would otherwise look like a long gap.
- **Vouched for to the goal.** Past its goal, a fast is only as long as the record can vouch for: a meal logged hours later may only mean meals went unlogged. So a fast ended by a meal, or still running, counts to its goal for the scale, the estimate and fasted days (only one ended by hand counts beyond it), and one with no meal logged 12 hours past its goal is taken as over at its goal. A 16-hour fast followed by a day not logged leaves that day unknown, as any unlogged day is.
- **Whole days inside a fast count as fasted days** without being marked, at what was drunk (under 50 kcal), as a day marked Fasted does (now also with under 50 kcal logged, where it needed nothing at all before). With the fast's other days they're left out of what the estimate learns from; their deficit is what's taken off the weigh-ins before the fast.
- **One fast at a time.** Starting one while one runs does nothing (Siri says you're already fasting); starting and ending both have Undo; a start can't be set before the last meal logged (that meal would end it at once). Turning meal timing off ends a running fast.
- **The scale around a long fast (a day or more).** Its weigh-ins and those of the 3 days after leave the trend shown; the maintenance estimate and the check-in learn as if those days were eaten at maintenance (they're left out, and the energy the fast went without comes off the weigh-ins before it); the check-in holds while a long fast runs and for 3 days after. In a simulated 72-hour fast this kept maintenance within 100 kcal of the truth, where the scale's 2 kg of water would have moved it 300 down and then 150 up. Today says the scale is water.
- **A running fast quiets the "protein to go" nudge** and "Yesterday is empty" for a fasted yesterday. Targets don't change during a fast, and the days after don't get the calories back.
- **Notifications** at 12 hours, 24 hours and 3 days (whichever fall before the goal) and at the goal, on by default once meal timing is on; the one at a day is about salt and minerals, the goal's about breaking the fast (an ordinary meal after a day; something small first after two; a doctor after five, for refeeding syndrome).
- **The Live Activity** starts with a fast and again each time the app is opened while one runs, since the system ends one after about 8 hours. The Lock Screen widget and the Watch complication don't need the app: their timelines step through the phases.
- **History's Overnight column** is the time from the evening before's last meal to the day's first, so the day a 2-day fast ends shows the whole fast.
- **Stadig Lift hears about a fast.** Stadig Macro's published summary carries the running fast (start and goal) while meal timing is on, as an optional field without a schema bump: readers that don't know it skip it, and a bump would stop older apps reading the file at all (Stadig Lift's steps and physique were added to its summary the same way). Stadig Lift says "Lifting on a long fast" when a strength session is still to do today outside a deload and the fast is 24 hours old now, or will be by the usual start (the middle of the last eight sessions' start times) while its goal still runs. Open: 24 hours as the line (some people lift fine fasted at 16), and a lighter session offered with a tap rather than only suggested.
- **Discipline on fasted days.** A whole day inside a fast is judged against that day's targets like a day marked Fasted already was, so it counts as a miss. Open: leaving days of a fast started on purpose out of Discipline, as vacation days are.

- **More meal request words.** "Just" ends a negation ("no pasta just rice"); "intolerant", "allergy" and "sensitive" after a food group, or "allergy to" before one, leave it out; "coeliac" is gluten-free; "busy" and "no time" are quicker; a food group is stored as the setup's word for it ("lactose" as dairy, "nut" as nuts), so its chip shows it left out; and words match whole, so "breakfast" no longer reads as "fast" and "peanut-free" no longer leaves out every nut. Open: whether "busy" should mean quicker.
- **Meal requests.** A negation carries over "and" through a list ("no fish and pork" avoids both), a verb such as use, try, make or have straight after "and" ends it ("no rice and use quinoa" wants quinoa), and an allergen named by its tag ("peanut", "soy", "eggs") avoids the tag. Open: whether that verb list is the right one.
- **Swap tips by name.** Names match whole words, so "chocolate" no longer reads as cola; cookies, brownies, doughnuts, candy and "chocolate chip" get the sweet-spot line, and a processed-meat name decides over the protein share (pastrami and sliced ham get the processed-meat tip, not the protein one). Open: whether lean cured meats should get the protein tip, as before.
- **Desserts and breads.** A cake, pie or tart with no keyword gets the sweet tip only when sugar is 35% or more of its calories, so a commercial apple pie (about 27%) gets the meal tip, and "hot dog buns", "hamburger buns" and "white sandwich bread" get the processed-meat or meal tip, since those checks run before bread. Open: naming dessert words (with savoury pies and fish or crab cakes sent to the meal tip) or a sugar share of about 25%, and checking bread words first when the name ends in one.
- **Weigh-ins deleted in Health.** A weigh-in deleted in Health now leaves Stadig Macro (only those read from Health, matched by sample id; typed ones never), unlike Stadig Lift's workouts, but one deleted before this device's first read of changes stays. Open: a one-time reconcile against what Health still has on that first read.

### Shared and the System

- **Private Cloud Compute stays off until Apple grants the entitlement.** Without Apple's managed `com.apple.developer.private-cloud-compute` entitlement, Foundation Models reports Private Cloud Compute as available and then stops the app on the first request, so SystemCore's `PrivateCloud.isEntitled` (the Info.plist key `SystemAIPrivateCloud`) gates every use: Stadig Macro's meal AI and meal-plan request reader. Stadig Macro hides its switch until then. Open: once the entitlement arrives, add `<key>SystemAIPrivateCloud</key><true/>` to each app's `Config/Info.plist` (and the entitlement itself).
- **UK units.** Only the US measurement system now defaults to pounds and inches; the UK and everywhere else get kilograms and centimetres (`UnitPreference.regional(for:)`); it's only the onboarding default, and Stadig Lift and Stadig Macro both have unit pickers. Open: inches for UK length, with weight still in kilograms.
- **Open-ended life modes.** Only an injury or illness can run "Until it's better"; a vacation or crunch always has an end, so all three editors offer the toggle only for those, and Save stays off for anything `LifeModePeriod.isValid` refuses (an end before the start, an open-ended trip or crunch, an injury with no part named). Open: letting a vacation or crunch run open-ended too, by relaxing `allowsOpenEnd`.
- **Life-mode editors.** All three save through SystemCore's `LifeModePeriod.edited(openEnded:)`, so a period given an end gets a week by default (Stadig Mind used to give it one day), and they hide the end button ("End today"; in Stadig Lift "End it now", "It's better: ramp back in" or "I'm better") until a period has started, so Delete cancels one still to come. Open: turning "End today" on a period still to come into a delete rather than hiding it.
- **Life-mode deletes.** A delete is a tombstone shared through `core/life-modes.json` and each app's store; the newest edit wins by `updatedAt` to the millisecond, a delete wins a tie, and tombstones are never pruned (a handful a year). The file and `LifeModeRecord` are at schema 2 in all three apps, so a build that only knows 1 can't write a deleted period back, but it also no longer sees periods a newer build saves. Open: pruning tombstones after about 90 days, which needs that threshold settled, and whether schema 2 everywhere is right.
- **Birth years.** A birth year that makes someone under 10 or over 100 counts as unknown (`SharedProfile.plausibleBirthYears`), so heart-rate zones, the step floor, endurance norms, micronutrient targets and the energy formula use the default age, 35; Stadig Lift won't save one outside that range, and Stadig Macro's onboarding picker offers only years inside it (it used to run from 1940 to 14 years ago). Open: whether 10 to 100 is the right range, with Stadig Macro's old 1940 to 14 years ago as the other candidate.
- **Restoring a backup.** A backup restores only into the app that wrote it and is refused before anything is written ("This backup is from Stadig Mind. Restore it there."), with no exception for the hub; `AppID.knowledge` now shows as "Stadig Mind". Open: taking what every app shares (the profile and life modes) from another app's backup, or accepting the hub's.
- **CSV exports.** A text field that starts like a formula (`=`, `+`, `-`, `@`, a tab or a return) gets a leading apostrophe, which Excel hides and Numbers shows; plain numbers such as -5 are left alone. Open: doing it only in Stadig Macro, whose food names come from Open Food Facts, or using another marker, such as a leading space.
- **A save against a delete.** In the record store, a save in the same millisecond as a delete wins (Stadig Macro deletes a day's state and saves it again under the same id), while imports, de-duplication and the life-mode merge let the delete win a tie; the pass tried making the delete win here too, and it lost those saves. Open: keeping that exception, or making a delete win every tie, with Stadig Macro no longer deleting a day's state before saving it.
- **Health on returning.** Health is read again each time the app comes back, at most every ten minutes in Stadig Lift and every five in Stadig Macro, and both start their Health observer at every launch, not only after "Connect Apple Health". Open: the intervals, and whether the two should match.
- **Test launches.** `-inMemoryStore`, `-uiTesting` and the unit-test host keep the shared container, the widget snapshot and Stadig Lift's `live-session.json` in a temporary folder, so a sample run never reaches the App Group, the other apps or the widgets, and the two flags work only in DEBUG builds. Open: letting `-inMemoryStore -seedSampleData` publish to the App Group again, so the other apps and the widgets show the sample.
- **UI test retries.** The generated UI test plans retry a failed test once (`retryOnFailure`, two runs at most), since a slow simulator frame can fail a good run, so a flaky test can pass without anyone noticing. Open: no retries, so every flake shows.
- **Days on other calendars.** A `Day` is now always the Gregorian date in the device's time zone; days saved on a phone set to another calendar before the fix sit at the wrong dates (Buddhist ones 543 years ahead), and there's no migration, since nothing has shipped. Open: a migration, if a test device ran another calendar.
- **Stadig Macro's food markers beyond the Log tab.** The same emoji square is on every list of foods and every screen a food opens on. A meal's row in the logger and "Same as" have a fork and knife in the square: a meal isn't one food. Open: whether "Same as" should show its foods' markers instead.
- **Search suggestions, and the model ordering them.** Suggestions are search text, not foods: a tap fills the search rather than logging. They come from the rows a search already found, so there's no second query per key, and spelling fixes and the person's foods carry through. The model runs on the device only, never Private Cloud Compute, even when that's allowed: it runs on what's being typed and on the person's log, and shouldn't spend quota or need a connection. It can only reorder, so it can't suggest what the search won't find. It has 2 seconds from a pause in the typing, and the chips can still move in that time. Open: whether they should move at all, or only put the model's first pick first.
